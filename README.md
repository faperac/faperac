Hi 👋 My name is Fabricio
=========================

Founder of RivieraConnect
-------------------------

42 Nice alumnus based on the French Riviera. I founded [**RivieraConnect**](https://rivieraconnect.fr/fr), a network integrator for homes and businesses in the Alpes-Maritimes: we design, install and maintain reliable internet and Wi-Fi, from the satellite dish to the server rack.

* 🛰️  **Starlink** installation, pro setups and remote sites
* 🗄️  **Network racks**: cabling, patch panels, switches, clean and labelled
* 📶  **UniFi** networks: gateways, switches, access points, centralised management
* 🔁  **Failover**: Starlink + fiber/4G/5G so the connection never drops
* 🧩  **VLANs** and segmentation: staff, guests, cameras, IoT kept apart
* 🔒  Guest Wi-Fi, firewall rules, VPN and remote monitoring
* 🌐  Also behind [Satellite Internet 06](https://satellite-internet-06.fr/) and [AzurWifi](https://azurwifi.fr/) to reach even more customers
* 🌍  I'm based in Nice
* ✉️  You can contact me at [fabricio@rivieraconnect.fr](mailto:fabricio@rivieraconnect.fr)
* 🍗  My favorite dish is still roasted chicken with rice.
